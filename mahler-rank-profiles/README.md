# 🔢 Reading Iwasawa λ-invariants from Mahler matrices modulo p — code and data

**Take a measure on $\mathbb Z_p$ that scales under $x\mapsto px$, and test its multiplicative translates against the
Mahler basis modulo $p$. Which rows of that matrix depend on the rows above them is decided, row by row, by the
$\lambda$-invariants of its Teichmüller components. Then take the ring generated by the symmetric Gaussian binomial
coefficients at a root of unity of order $p^vn$: its graded layers, character by character, show the same profile,
with the $\lambda$-invariants of the $p$-adic $L$-functions $L_p(s,\chi\omega^{b+1})$.**

📦 **Programs, archived outputs, data and Lean files for the paper *Reading Iwasawa $\lambda$-invariants from
Mahler matrices modulo $p$* (C. Marín, 2026). Every number in its Section 7 is produced by a program here.**

## 🧭 The objects

**Rows.** $p$ odd, $k$ a field of characteristic $p$, $\mu$ a $k$-valued measure on $\mathbb Z_p$ with
$\mu(pX)=\rho\,\mu(X)$, $\rho\neq0$. The matrix is $B_{J,x}=\int\binom{xy}{J}d\mu(y)$, $0\le J,x<p^a$. For each
$b\bmod p-1$, $r_b$ is the order at $T=0$ of $f_b(T)=\int_{\mathbb Z_p^\times}\omega(y)^b(1+T)^{\vartheta(y)}d\mu(y)$.

**Layers.** $p\ge5$, $n\ge3$ odd with $p\nmid n\varphi(n)$, $a\ge2$, $2m+1=p^an$, $Q=p^vn$ with $v$ large enough. The
coefficients of $\prod_{j=-m}^{m}(1+\zeta_Q^{2j}T)$ generate an order $A\subseteq\mathbb Z[\zeta_Q+\zeta_Q^{-1}]$; its
graded layers $G_i$ for the radical filtration over $\mathbb Z_p$ split by Dirichlet characters mod $n$.

**The measure of a character.** For $\chi$ odd primitive mod $n$, $\mu_\chi$ has moments
$\int x^kd\mu_\chi=-2(1-\chi(2)2^k)L(-k,\chi)$: the measure of $\chi$ with the Euler factor at $2$ removed.

## 🎯 What is proved (in the paper)

1. **Theorem A.** For $0<J<p^a$ with $J+1=p^su$, $p\nmid u$, $b\equiv J\bmod p-1$: row $J$ is dependent
   $\iff u<p\,r_b$. No bound on $r_b$, every Teichmüller component. Row $0$ is dependent $\iff\mu(\mathbb Z_p)=0$.
2. **Consequences.** The status of a row is invariant under $\Phi(J)=p(J+1)-1$; the first new row along
   $J_k=p^{a-2}(k(p-1)+1)-1$ is at $k^*=\lceil pr_0/(p-1)\rceil$; and
   $\operatorname{rank}B=[\mu(\mathbb Z_p)\neq0]+\sum_b\sum_{t<a}\max(p^t-r_b,0)$.
3. **Theorem B.** For $0\le j<p^a-p^{a-1}$, the $\chi$-component of $G_{p^a+j}$ vanishes $\iff$ row $j$ is dependent
   for $\mu_\chi$. For even $b$, $r_b(\mu_\chi)=\lambda_p(\chi\omega^{b+1})+[\chi(2)\omega(2)^b=1]\,p^{v_p(\vartheta(2))}$.
4. **Corollaries C and D.** Along the $p-1$ layers $p^a+J_k$ the components vanish exactly at the first
   $\min(\tilde\lambda,p-1)$; along the layers $p^a+j_{0,i}$, $j_{b,i}=pi+[b-i]_{p-1}$, they vanish exactly at the
   first $\tilde\lambda$, provided $\tilde\lambda<p^{a-1}-p^{a-2}$.

For $j\le p-2$, Theorem B is a twisted form of Kummer's criterion (Coates–Wiles homomorphisms; Girstmair's character
coordinates of cosecant numbers). What is new there is the identification of layers with rows.

## 🧪 How to doubt a number

Every program starts with a header that says what it computes, why, and how to run it. Read the archived output, or
run the program again. Several checks carry a **control that must fail**: `semillas_rango.py` perturbs $r_0$ and must
disagree; the sixteen targets of `objetivos_ciegos.csv` were chosen by their $\lambda$ alone, computed from
generalized Bernoulli numbers, before any layer was read.

## ⚖️ What is not here, and what is not claimed

- **Not covered:** non-primitive characters; the non-semisimple case $p\mid\varphi(n)$; the layers beyond
  $p^a-p^{a-1}$.
- **Not read:** N. Childress, *The coefficients of a $p$-adic measure and its $\Gamma$-transform*, Manuscripta Math.
  116 (2005). The paper says so in its comparison with the literature.
- **Held out by location:** working notes do not travel with this folder; a check refuses to
  build it if anything here imports or reads a file that is not in it.
