GL(n) at a torsion element* (Carles Marín), deposited on Zenodo:
[doi:10.5281/zenodo.22847476](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22847476). That is the **concept DOI**

## 🎯 What the note proves

Evaluate every irreducible character of a compact simple group at the principal element
$g_q=\exp(2\pi i\rho/q)$. The values generate an order $A$ in the ring of integers $\mathcal{O}$ of
their field.

1. **Three operations, one lemma.** Multiplying a monic polynomial by a monic integer factor does
   not change the ring generated by its coefficients. On the segment of exponents this gives three
   operations that leave $A$ unchanged: adjoining an eigenvalue $1$, which makes the ring of
   $\mathrm{SU}(2m+1)$ equal to that of $\mathrm{Sp}(2m)$; the complement $n\mapsto q-n$, which is
   level-rank duality as an equality of orders; and the period $n\mapsto n+q$. So $A$ depends only
   on the segment.
2. **The local picture.** At $p$, the index of a stable segment is the sum of its layer defects,
   and the relative class number governs the first layer.
3. **Which primes.** A prime $p$ divides $[\mathcal{O}:A]$ only if the prime-to-$p$ part $r$ of $q$
   divides $n-1$, $n$ or $n+1$; inside these families it does whenever $p$ is an odd prime dividing
   $q$ and $\varphi(r)\ge4$.
4. **The non-real ring of $\mathrm{GL}(n)$.** With $n=Nr$ or $Nr+1$, $2\le n<q$, $r\ge5$ odd and $p$
   odd, the order is locally a free quadratic extension of the ring of the recentred segment.

## 🧪 How to doubt a number

```
python segmento.py autotest
```

recomputes from nothing the worked examples the note prints — layer profiles, indices, the shortcut
against the slow path, two counts — exercises every guard, and ends with `SELF-TEST: all correct`.
Every other script says in its header what it computes; read the `*_OUT.txt` beside it or run it
again. The `.sage` files run under SageMath 10.x.
